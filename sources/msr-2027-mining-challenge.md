# MSR 2027 Mining Challenge

Short overview only — details live on the challenge page and in [[specmine-large-scale-corpus-of-spec-driven-development-artifacts]].

## Key points

- Two datasets: **GitSkills** (agent skills) and **SpecMine** (SDD artefacts); use either or both.
- SpecMine focus: adoption/diffusion; anatomy/quality of specs; spec–code link; human–AI authorship; lifecycle/evolution/abandonment.
- Deadlines (AoE): abstract **2026-12-18**; paper **2026-12-23**; notification **2027-01-19**; camera-ready **2027-01-26**. HotCRP: https://msr2027-challenge.hotcrp.com/
- Short papers in IEEE Xplore (4+1 pages). Cite dataset version (e.g. Zenodo DOI); open science expected.
